# WatchBuddy — Account Deletion

You can delete your WatchBuddy account and all data associated with it at any
time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

## In the app (instant)

1. Open WatchBuddy, go to the **Profile** tab and tap **Edit Profile**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account…**
3. Confirm twice. Your profile, library, watch history, ratings, reviews, favorites
   follows, blocks and the reports you filed are erased and you are signed out.

## By request

If you can no longer access the app, email **bunescurobert16@gmail.com** (subject: "Delete my
account") from the address you signed up with, or tell us that address in the
message. Please don't post it in a public GitHub issue. Deletion requests are
honored within 30 days.

## What is deleted

Everything tied to your account: sign-in identity (email), profile (name,
username, bio, avatar), library entries, watch history, ratings, reviews,
favorites, follow relationships, blocks and the reports you filed. Your
avatar image is deleted from storage too.

One thing is kept: a report someone else filed about your review, reply or
profile stays on our side, so we can still act on abuse. It holds the
reporter's reason and a reference to content that no longer exists — none of
your profile or activity.
